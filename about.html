About Assimilation

Healthcare data exchange, done with care

Assimilation Technology has been transforming healthcare data exchange since 2012. Our team is committed to improving the exchange of healthcare data in hospital and provider systems.

Our work

We help hospitals, physician offices, and health information networks move clinical and operational data between systems. That work is integration and automation: interfaces, APIs, and the project execution required to put them into production.

The public website states more than 18 years of experience in healthcare data exchange. The company has operated under the Assimilation Technology name since 2012.

Assimilation LLC is a Texas domestic limited liability company (file date 17 August 2014). David Jarvis is the registered agent and is listed as owner, director, and manager in Texas public filings.
